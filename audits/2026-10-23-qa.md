### QA-004: Sign-up error messages are lost in production
- **Area:** Customer sign-up
- **URL:** https://kinetic.openship.org/schedule?book=cmv5jn8cn001onv01nmxchzm2 (Create account), also `/join` and `/account`
- **Steps:** Create an account with an email that already exists (a different password).
- **Expected:** "An account with that email already exists. Sign in instead, or reset your password…"
- **Actual:** "Your account could not be created. Try again, or contact the club." The API returns `{"message":"Unexpected error."}` for `registerMember`, because Keystone masks plain `Error`s in production. So none of the mapped messages in `signUpFailure` (duplicate email, too-common password, rate limit, signups off) ever reach customers.
- **Likely file:** `features/keystone/mutations/memberRegistration.ts:58,65` (throw `GraphQLError` with an `extensions.code`), `features/storefront/lib/data/user.ts:281` (map on the code). Check other custom mutations (`bookClass`, freeze/cancel) for the same pattern.

## Business sense (day-one gaps for a real gym)
- No rolling timetable (QA-003) and no waiver (QA-007) are the two things a gym can't open with.
- No working email (QA-005): no password reset, booking confirmations, receipts or waitlist promotion notices.
- Drop-ins, day passes and trials aren't sold online ("arranged by the front desk"). Most boutique gyms sell an intro offer online; it is the main funnel for new members.
- Plan changes are "made by the front desk"; there's no self-serve upgrade from Basic to Unlimited.
- The dashboard is untested this round. Re-run with a working staff login to cover members, check-in, rosters, scheduling, billing and reports.
